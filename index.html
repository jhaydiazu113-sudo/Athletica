Privacy Policy & Terms

Athletica is a student project store operated by the group members listed in our footer. This page explains what information we collect and how orders are handled.

Information we collect
When you place an order, we ask for your name, contact number, delivery address, and a screenshot of your payment confirmation. This information is used only to process and deliver your order, and is never sold or shared with third parties.

Payment
We currently accept manual payment confirmation through GCash, Maya, PayPal, or Cash on Delivery. We never ask for your full card number, bank PIN, or one-time passwords (OTP). If anyone asks you for these while claiming to be Athletica, please contact us immediately using the details on our Contact page.

Returns & refunds
If an item arrives damaged or incorrect, message us within 3 days of delivery with your order details and photos of the item, and we will arrange a replacement or refund.

Questions
Reach out through our Contact page for anything not covered here.
